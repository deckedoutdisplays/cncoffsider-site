Delete your account

In the app (instant)

You → Sync across devices → Manage → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything synced to it.

Without the app

Email deckedoutdisplays@proton.me from the address you signed up with, asking for your account to be deleted. We'll delete the account and all data synced to it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What's deleted

Your email address, password, and all synced data: machine settings, bits, materials, projects, cut logs, maintenance tasks, Cost & Pricing entries, tuning reports and File Check results. Copies in routine database backups expire on their normal schedule.

Data stored only on your device isn't affected — remove it with Reset all data in the app, or by uninstalling.
