Privacy policy

Last updated 4 October 2026.

The short version

Without an account, everything you enter stays on your device. The only thing the app sends is a check of whether you have Pro, under a random ID (see Purchases).

If you create an account to sync between devices, your data is stored with our hosting provider so your other devices can download it. We don’t sell it, show ads, or track you.

What we collect

Account: your email address and password (stored hashed by our authentication provider — we never see it).

Your shop data, only when signed in: machine settings, bits, materials, projects, cut logs, maintenance tasks, Cost & Pricing entries, “how did it run?” reports, and File Check results — the summary numbers, verdict and file name. The G-code files themselves are read on your device and never uploaded.

We don’t collect location, contacts, analytics, or advertising identifiers.

How it’s used

Only to sync your data between your own devices and keep your account working. It is never shared with other users, sold, or used for advertising.

Purchases

If you buy Pro, Apple or Google handles the payment — we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your account has Pro. It receives a random app ID (or, when you’re signed in, your account’s ID) and your purchase history for this app, nothing else.

Where it’s stored

With Supabase, our database and authentication provider. Data is encrypted in transit, and database rules only let each account read and write its own rows.

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time from You → Sync across devices → Delete account. That permanently removes the account and everything synced to it. Data on your device stays there until you remove it (You → Backup & data → Reset all data) or uninstall the app.

You can also ask us to delete your account at deckedoutdisplays@proton.me.

Children

CNC Offsider isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be in the app with a new date.

Questions: deckedoutdisplays@proton.me.

Terms of use

Estimates, not guarantees

Feeds, speeds, depths and File Check verdicts are estimates from published manufacturer data and CNC Offsider’s own model. They can be wrong for your bits, material or machine. Always check against your tooling manufacturer’s guidance, start conservatively, and stay with the machine. You are responsible for how your machine is set up and run.

A File Check “all-clear” means CNC Offsider found nothing it knows to flag. It can’t see your workholding, bit condition, grain, or machine wear.

Your responsibility

CNC machines can cause serious injury and damage. Follow your machine and tooling manufacturers’ safety instructions, wear appropriate protection, and never leave a running machine unattended.

Liability

To the extent the law allows, CNC Offsider is provided “as is”, without warranties, and we aren’t liable for damage to machines, tooling, material or property, or for injury, arising from use of the app’s estimates. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law or other laws that can’t be excluded.

Subscriptions

CNC Offsider Pro is available as a monthly or yearly subscription. Your first three File Checks are free and run in full.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and are charged to your Apple ID or Google account. A free trial, where offered, turns into a paid subscription when it ends unless cancelled before then. Manage or cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings — deleting the app doesn’t cancel a subscription.

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

Your data is yours. See the privacy policy for how it’s handled and how to delete it.
